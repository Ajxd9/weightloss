Welcome to Daily Progress
A private, offline food, weight and workout log. No account, no cloud sync — everything you enter stays on this device. Nothing here is pre-filled with anyone's real data; enter your own to get started.
Daily targets
Set your own daily calorie and macro targets — nothing is pre-filled. Not sure what to use? A common, gentle starting point is roughly 15–20% below your maintenance calories with protein around 1.6–2g per kg of goal weight. Adjust anytime in Settings.
Ready to go
No history is pre-filled — your diary starts empty today. A default strength + cardio routine is ready in the Workout tab, and you can swap in whichever exercises you actually want from "Choose exercises" any time. Steps sync, barcode scanning and phone notifications are later-stage additions; food logging, weight, measurements and workouts work fully offline right now.
